SDAI connects to popular AI providers including AUTOMATIC1111, SwarmUI, AI Horde, Hugging Face Inference, OpenAI, Stability AI, Fal.ai, ArliAI, and Local Diffusion engines.

Local generation spans Android ONNX, Google AI MediaPipe, stable-diffusion.cpp SDXL, iOS Silicon Diffusion Core ML, and PrismML Bonsai Image 4B on supported devices.

Keep the controls artists expect: positive and negative prompts, size, seed, CFG scale, sampling steps, restore faces, Img2Img, Inpaint, LoRA, embeddings, hypernetworks, and model selection where supported.
